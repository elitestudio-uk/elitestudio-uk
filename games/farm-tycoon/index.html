Farm Tycoon · Management Sim

From one field to a working estate.

Plan your crops, invest in the right machinery and read the seasons. Farm Tycoon is a management sim about the long game — building something that endures, one harvest at a time.

The Game

Every acre is a decision.

Inherit a tired smallholding and a stubborn tractor. Choose what to plant and when, rotate your fields to keep the soil healthy, and time your sales to catch the market at its best. Expand into livestock, greenhouses and processing — or stay lean and specialise. The estate you end up with is entirely yours.

Farm Tycoon shares Carp Tycoon's philosophy: systems deep enough to master, presented with restraint. No timers nagging you to spend. Just a satisfying loop of plan, plant, grow and reinvest.

Features

What you'll be managing

Crop rotation

Balance cash crops against soil health. Rotate wisely or watch your yields fall away over the years.

Machinery & upgrades

From a single tractor to a full fleet of harvesters, drills and balers. Every upgrade changes how much land you can work.

Market timing

Prices move with supply and demand. Store your harvest and sell at the peak, or lock in a contract for certainty.

Seasons & weather

Frost, drought and rain shape every year. Adapt your plan or take the hit — no two seasons play the same.

Livestock & expansion

Add cattle, sheep and pigs, build greenhouses, or move into processing to add value beyond the raw crop.

Long-term progression

Grow from a struggling smallholding to a respected estate. Deep systems that reward planning, not impatience.
